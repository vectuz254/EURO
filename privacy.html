Privacy Policy

Last updated: [Month Year]

Joint Hands Charity ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you give us directly, such as:

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you contact us, donate, or sign up as a member
  • Account details (name, email, password) when you register for the members area — passwords are stored securely by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us in plain text
  • Donation details necessary to process M-Pesa, bank, or international transfers — we do not store card or M-Pesa PIN information ourselves
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used to improve the site

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the members area and respond to enquiries
  • To process and acknowledge donations
  • To share programme updates with members who opt in
  • To improve our website and services

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Member account data is stored with Firebase (a Google Cloud service) using industry-standard security practices. We take reasonable steps to protect your information but cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers (such as Firebase/Google, or payment processors) solely to operate the site and process donations, and where required by law.

5. International Operations

Joint Hands Charity works with partners and supporters in Kenya, Europe, and the United States. Where we transfer information internationally, we take steps to keep it protected consistent with this policy.

6. Your Choices

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us
  • You may close your member account at any time by contacting us
  • You may opt out of email updates at any time

7. Cookies

Our site may use minimal cookies or local storage required for the members area to function (such as keeping you logged in). We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
